GUIDE

From installation to your first order.

Step by step – how to get Gravio Plus ready in your business.

Gravio Plus manual (PDF, German) Gravio Plus Dispo manual (PDF, German)

1 · Install the app

  1. On the Android phone, tap “Download APK” below.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, turn on “Allow from this source”.
  4. Tap “Install” and open Gravio Plus.

2 · Set up your company

  1. Menu (⋮ top right) → Company details & logo: enter logo, company name and footer.
  2. Enter dispatch’s WhatsApp number – every job sheet will go there.
  3. Choose the language of the app and of the PDF.

3 · Adjust lists & fields

  1. Menu → Lists & fields: add your own vehicles, trailers and equipment.
  2. Hide fields you don’t need, add your own pick lists.
  3. Tip: vehicles with “Kran” (crane) in the name automatically show crane work.

4 · Design the job sheet

  1. Menu → PDF builder: switch sections on or off and reorder them.
  2. Set the accent colour, document title and your own note.
  3. Check the preview with the eye icon at the top.

5 · Fill in your first order

  1. Enter the header data or open the order from dispatch via its WhatsApp link.
  2. Fill in the trip: times, waiting time, services, photos.
  3. Tap the signature field and let the customer sign.

6 · Send & activate

  1. Green send icon: the job sheet goes to dispatch as a PDF.
  2. Every trip automatically lands in the Trip archive (Menu → Archive), where you can find it any time.
  3. After the trial, enter the activation code in the menu under Activate. To renew, paste the new code the same way.
